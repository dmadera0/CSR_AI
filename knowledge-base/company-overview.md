# Demosite Company Overview

## About Demosite
Demosite was founded in 2018 with a mission to make high-quality 
products and exceptional service accessible to everyone. We serve 
over 10,000 satisfied customers across 15 countries with a catalog 
of 500+ products.

## Key Facts
- Founded: 2018 (7 years in business)
- Customers: 10,000+
- Products: 500+
- Countries served: 15

## Mission & Values
- Mission: Deliver exceptional value through premium products, 
  transparent pricing, and unwavering customer support
- Innovation: Continuously improve services and embrace technology
- Integrity: Honesty and transparency in all business conduct
- Customer First: Every decision guided by customer satisfaction
- Sustainability: Responsible sourcing and eco-friendly packaging
- Excellence: Pursue excellence in every aspect of the business
