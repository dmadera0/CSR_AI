# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Times
- Standard (all plans): 3-5 business days, free
- Express (Pro/Enterprise): 1-2 business days, free
- Same-day: Enterprise local orders only

## Free Shipping
All plans include free shipping applied automatically at checkout.

## Order Processing
Weekend and holiday orders begin processing the next business day.

## Order Tracking
Tracking number sent by email after shipment. Also view in
Account → Order History.

## Shipping Countries
Ships to 15 countries. Email support@demosite.com to confirm availability.

## Address Changes
Must be requested within 2 hours of ordering.
Call 1-800-DEMO-123 for urgent address changes.
