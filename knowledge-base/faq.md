# Demosite Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders & Shipping

Q: How do I place an order?
A: Browse products, click "Add to Cart," then proceed to checkout. 
Enter your shipping address and payment information. You will receive 
an order confirmation email with tracking information within 24 hours.

Q: What are your shipping times?
A: Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping 
(Pro and Enterprise members) takes 1-2 business days. Orders placed 
on weekends or holidays begin processing the next business day.

Q: Do you offer free shipping?
A: Yes. All Standard Plan and higher members receive free shipping 
on all orders. Free shipping is applied automatically at checkout.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: After your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation 
email with a tracking number. You can also track orders from your 
Demosite account under "Order History."

Q: What areas do you ship to?
A: We ship to 15 countries across North America, Europe, and Asia. 
Enter your address at checkout to confirm delivery availability. 
Contact support@demosite.com for countries not listed.

Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
A: Address changes must be requested within 2 hours of placing your 
order. Contact support immediately with your order number and new 
address. Call 1-800-DEMO-123 for urgent changes.

## Returns & Refunds

Q: What is your return policy?
A: Standard Plan: 30 days. Pro Plan: 60 days. Enterprise Plan: 
90 days. Items must be unused, in original condition, with all 
original packaging. Return shipping is free for qualifying returns.

Q: How do I return an item?
A: Log into your account, select "Return Item" from your order, 
and print the prepaid return label. Drop it off at any carrier 
location. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of 
receiving your return.

Q: Are there items I cannot return?
A: Clearance items marked "Final Sale," custom orders, personalized 
or engraved items, and items damaged due to misuse cannot be returned.

Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving your 
return. Allow an additional 3-5 business days for it to appear on 
your account statement.

Q: What if my item arrives damaged?
A: Contact us within 48 hours with photos of the damage. We will 
arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost. Call 1-800-DEMO-123 
for immediate assistance.

## Account & Billing

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click "Get Started" on the homepage. Enter your email, create a 
password, and verify your email. Your account is ready immediately.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Your Password?" on the login page. Enter your 
email and click the reset link we send you. Links expire after 24 hours.

Q: Do you offer subscription services?
A: Yes. The Pro Plan is $9.99/month and includes priority shipping, 
60-day returns, express delivery, phone support, and member discounts. 
Cancel anytime from account settings.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, 
and Google Pay. All payments use 256-bit SSL encryption.

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliance. We 
never store full credit card numbers on our servers.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Log in, go to "Subscription Settings," and click "Cancel 
Subscription." Benefits continue until the end of your billing cycle. 
Email support@demosite.com or call 1-800-DEMO-123 for help.
