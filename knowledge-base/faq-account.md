# FAQ: Account & Billing

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click "Get Started", enter email, create password, verify email.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password?" on login page. Reset link valid for 24 hours.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay.

Q: Is my payment info secure?
A: Yes. 256-bit SSL encryption, PCI-DSS compliant. Card numbers never stored.

Q: What is the Pro Plan subscription?
A: $9.99/month. Includes priority shipping, 60-day returns, phone support,
and member discounts. Cancel anytime.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Account → Subscription Settings → Cancel Subscription.
Benefits last until end of billing cycle.
