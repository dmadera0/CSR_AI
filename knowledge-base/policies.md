# Demosite Policies & Support

## Customer Support
- Email: support@demosite.com
- Phone: 1-800-DEMO-123
- Hours: Monday-Friday 8am-8pm EST
- Weekend: Saturday-Sunday 10am-6pm EST
- 24/7 support available for Pro and Enterprise plan members

## Shipping Policy
- Free shipping on all Standard Plan and above orders
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days (Pro/Enterprise)
- Same-day delivery: available for Enterprise (local)
- International: ships to 15 countries, customs duties may apply
- Weekend/holiday orders begin processing next business day

## Return Policy
- Standard Plan: 30-day return window
- Pro Plan: 60-day return window  
- Enterprise Plan: 90-day return window
- Items must be unused and in original packaging
- Return shipping: free for qualifying returns
- Refund timeline: 5-7 business days after warehouse receipt
- Non-returnable: Final Sale items, custom orders, personalized items,
  items damaged by misuse

## Damaged Items Policy
- Report damage within 48 hours of delivery
- Include photos of the damage in your report
- Resolution: replacement or full refund at no cost
- Most claims resolved within 24 hours

## Payment & Security
- Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, 
  Google Pay
- Security: 256-bit SSL encryption, PCI-DSS compliant
- Card numbers never stored on Demosite servers

## Privacy
- Personal information never shared with third parties without consent
- Full details in Privacy Policy on website
