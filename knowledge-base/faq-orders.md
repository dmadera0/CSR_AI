# FAQ: Orders & Shipping

Q: How do I place an order?
A: Browse products, click Add to Cart, then checkout. Enter shipping
address and payment. Confirmation email sent within 24 hours.

Q: What are shipping times?
A: Standard: 3-5 business days. Express (Pro/Enterprise): 1-2 days.

Q: Is shipping free?
A: Yes. All plans include free shipping applied automatically at checkout.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Via tracking email after shipment, or in Account → Order History.

Q: Where do you ship?
A: 15 countries. Email support@demosite.com to confirm your country.

Q: Can I change my shipping address?
A: Within 2 hours of ordering only. Call 1-800-DEMO-123 for urgent changes.
