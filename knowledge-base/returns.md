# Returns & Refunds

## Return Windows
- Standard Plan: 30 days
- Pro Plan: 60 days
- Enterprise Plan: 90 days

Items must be unused and in original packaging.
Return shipping is free for qualifying returns.

## How to Return
Log in → Order History → Return Item → print prepaid label → drop off.
Refunds processed within 5-7 business days after warehouse receives item.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Final Sale / clearance items
- Custom or personalized orders
- Items damaged by misuse

## Damaged Items
Report damage within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
We replace or issue a full refund at no cost.
Call 1-800-DEMO-123 for immediate assistance.
