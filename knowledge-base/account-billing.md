# Account & Billing

## Create Account
Click "Get Started" → enter email → create password → verify email.
Account is ready immediately after verification.

## Reset Password
Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter email, click the
reset link sent to you. Links expire after 24 hours.

## Payment Methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay,
Google Pay. All payments use 256-bit SSL encryption.

## Payment Security
PCI-DSS compliant. Full credit card numbers are never stored on servers.

## Pro Subscription — $9.99/month
Cancel anytime: Account → Subscription Settings → Cancel Subscription.
Benefits continue until the end of the current billing cycle.
